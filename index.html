Import Trades

Import your trade history directly from your trading platform. We auto-detect the format and map all fields.
Choose Your Platform
📊
MetaTrader 4 / MT5Export history from MT4/MT5 → Reports → Save as CSV
📈
TradingViewExport from TradingView Paper Trading or broker connection
⚡
cTraderExport history from cTrader → History → Export to CSV
📁
Generic CSVAny broker CSV with columns: Date, Pair, Direction, Lot, Entry, Exit, P&L
How to Export from Each Platform
MT4/MT5: Open Terminal → Account History tab → Right-click → Save as Report → Choose CSV format
TradingView: Go to Trade History in your broker connection → Export button → Download CSV
cTrader: History tab → Select date range → Export to CSV button at the bottom
Generic: Your CSV needs at minimum: Date, Symbol/Pair, Type (Buy/Sell), Volume/Lots, Open Price, Close Price, Profit/P&L
